# GoldenCell Investor Demo

## Purpose
Interactive investor-meeting prototype covering both sides of the GoldenCell marketplace.

## Demo flow

### Patient — 6 minutes
1. HOME — explain three independent discovery routes.
2. Direct Search — briefly show hospital/program discovery.
3. AI Match — enter patient needs and show personalized match results.
4. Treatment Bid — submit the same needs and show incoming hospital proposals.
5. AI Compare — demonstrate optional AI assistance after proposals arrive.
6. Consultation → My GoldenCell → Recovery.

### Hospital — 4 minutes
1. Hospital Dashboard.
2. Open the same request `GC-260814-0028` created in the patient journey.
3. Review structured Patient Treatment Needs.
4. Select a registered program and send a treatment consultation proposal.
5. Show consultation conversion and monthly channel performance.

## Investor talking points
- Direct Search, AI Match, and Treatment Bid are independent patient entry routes.
- AI supports discovery/comparison and does not diagnose or determine treatment.
- Treatment Bid is not designed as lowest-price competition; hospitals propose registered consultation programs against structured patient needs.
- All three routes converge into consultation/reservation and eventually Recovery.
- Hospital value is visible as patient acquisition performance, not only advertising impressions.
- Initial B2B monetization centers on advertising/program promotion and Review CMS; later expansion can include Hospital SaaS and analytics.

## Run locally
Open `index.html` directly in a browser. No build step or backend is required for this investor prototype.
